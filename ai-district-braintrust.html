The loop. Lab commitments, such as OpenAI’s $22.4B with CoreWeave and Anthropic’s $41.3B with Lambda, back the debt that neoclouds use to buy NVIDIA systems. NVIDIA in turn puts capital and demand guarantees back into those neoclouds ($6.3B + $2B for CoreWeave, $2B for Nebius, $1.5B for Lambda). The same dollar of AI demand shows up in lab commitments, neocloud backlog, and NVIDIA revenue.
